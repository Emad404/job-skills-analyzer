Career Roadmap
  1. 1

    Master foundational SQL — write queries, joins, aggregations, and window functions against real datasets from platforms like Mode Analytics or StrataScratch.

  2. 2

    Learn Python for data work: Pandas for manipulation, Matplotlib and Seaborn for visualization, and NumPy for numerical computing.

  3. 3

    Build 2–3 end-to-end portfolio projects that demonstrate data collection, cleaning, analysis, and storytelling with a public-facing dashboard or report.

  4. 4

    Earn the Google Data Analytics or IBM Data Analyst Professional Certificate to validate your skills and signal readiness to employers.

  5. 5

    Learn a BI tool deeply — Tableau or Power BI — and publish interactive dashboards that demonstrate stakeholder-ready communication of findings.

  6. 6

    Develop domain expertise in a specific industry (e-commerce, healthcare, finance) to position yourself as more than a generalist analyst.

  7. 7

    Apply for junior analyst or analyst intern roles, practice SQL interviews on LeetCode and case study interviews on platforms like Exponent.
